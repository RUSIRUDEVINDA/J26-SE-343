# Grade E - Financially unsuitable - over capacity with default history

A salaried applicant receives LKR 80,000 monthly but already repays LKR 40,000. Adding a LKR 20,000 lease consumes 75% of income, exceeding the 60% cap. Current severe arrears and a historical written-off facility add major risk.

- Monthly verified income: LKR 80,000.00
- Existing monthly debt repayments: LKR 40,000.00
- Requested monthly lease: LKR 20,000.00
- Debt + lease / income: 75%
- Daily average bank balance: LKR 29,393.44
- 60% maximum total monthly payments: LKR 48,000.00
- Available monthly lease capacity: LKR 8,000.00
- Bureau subgrade (synthetic): E1
- Project lease grade / total score: E / 0
- Expected action: Reject

## Bank statement evidence

Six monthly salary credits of LKR 80,000 and LKR 40,000 in existing debt payments. Each month opens and closes at LKR 35,000. The daily average balance is much smaller than in the other scenarios. No overdraft is needed for this applicant to be financially unsuitable.

## CRIB report evidence

Synthetic bureau grade E1. Active monthly installments are LKR 24,000 and LKR 16,000. Facility 1 has LKR 72,000 arrears and current NDIA of 120 days. A previously closed facility has a synthetic LKR 50,000 write-off; the tester verifies default history as true.

## Why this grade

Combined payments of 75% earn zero debt-service points. Stable income earns 25 and liquidity earns 5, while credit family E earns zero. The default penalty subtracts 50; the score is clamped to zero. The capacity rule also fails by LKR 12,000 per month.

## What this scenario tests

Grade E can occur despite a regular salary and a positive bank balance. Excessive obligations and verified adverse history drive the result.

Income consistency and default history are explicit reviewer-verified fixture inputs. Savings transfers are not expenses. Bureau scores are assigned test values; these documents do not imply official approval or live OCR success.
