# Grade C - Borderline - irregular repayment behaviour

A salaried applicant receives LKR 100,000 monthly and already pays LKR 25,000 toward loans. Adding the LKR 20,000 lease uses 45% of income. The credit report shows irregular repayment history, including a prior 35-day delinquency and a current 15-day arrears record.

- Monthly verified income: LKR 100,000.00
- Existing monthly debt repayments: LKR 25,000.00
- Requested monthly lease: LKR 20,000.00
- Debt + lease / income: 45%
- Daily average bank balance: LKR 76,196.72
- 60% maximum total monthly payments: LKR 60,000.00
- Available monthly lease capacity: LKR 35,000.00
- Bureau subgrade (synthetic): C1
- Project lease grade / total score: C / 55
- Expected action: ManualReview

## Bank statement evidence

Six equal monthly salary credits of LKR 100,000 and LKR 25,000 in monthly debt payments. The opening and closing balance is LKR 80,000. Salary is stable; the weakness here is repayment behaviour and combined commitments.

## CRIB report evidence

Synthetic bureau grade C1. Monthly installments are LKR 15,000 and LKR 10,000. Facility 1 has LKR 7,500 arrears and current NDIA of 15 days; the highest NDIA in the twelve-month profile is 35 days. Facility 2 is current.

## Why this grade

Combined commitments of 45% of income earn only 10 debt-service points. Income consistency earns 25, liquidity earns 15, and credit family C earns 5. No default penalty is assumed. Total: 55.

## What this scenario tests

The 60% capacity test passes, yet repayment irregularities and weaker debt-service scoring lead to manual review. Passing affordability does not establish low credit risk.

Income consistency and default history are explicit reviewer-verified fixture inputs. Savings transfers are not expenses. Bureau scores are assigned test values; these documents do not imply official approval or live OCR success.
