# Prospective Synthetic Family Review

This package is reserved for prospective synthetic testing after human review is frozen. No classifier was loaded or invoked, no model predictions were generated, and Experiment 5 outcomes were not used to recommend labels.

The proposed label and scenario grounding below are source metadata, not human decisions. All decision fields are intentionally blank.

## Reviewer instructions

Allowed Review_Decision values: Approved, Label_Change, Rejected, Needs_Discussion.
Review all three complete variants before deciding. Confirm semantic consistency independently; the structural check only records that the generator grouped the variants under one family and grounding.
Do not run prediction on this population until the human decisions have been completed and frozen.
